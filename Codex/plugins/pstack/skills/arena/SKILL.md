# Arena

Read ../../references/codex-runtime.md. Define the problem, constraints, acceptance checks, and scoring rubric before drafting candidates. Produce at least two structurally distinct approaches. Delegate candidate generation when available and authorized, with isolated outputs; otherwise generate candidates sequentially and label the comparison.

Check every candidate against the same observable requirements. A separate reviewer can assess the completed candidates read-only. Inherit the parent model unless explicit instructions choose an available alternative; do not assert model-family diversity without it. Verify scores against actual artifacts, select or synthesize a base, explain concrete tradeoffs, and validate the result. Treat unsupported capabilities and untested claims as gaps.
