# Why

Read ../../references/codex-runtime.md and references/epistemics.md. Anchor the question to code, symbols, revisions, blame, history, and linked PRs or issues. Discover available connectors through current tools. Search relevant categories: source history, issue tracker, long-form documents, team chat, infrastructure, error tracking, and product analytics. Use the matching references/sources/ playbook and references/investigator-prompt.md. Delegate when available and authorized or investigate sequentially.

Record null results and unavailable sources. Connected reading does not authorize sending messages. Synthesize with references/synthesizer-prompt.md, spot-check citations, and separate what was found, reasonable inference, competing explanations, and what remains unknown. For planned changes, translate supported rationale into preserve/change/avoid/risk constraints. Do not treat implementation shape as proof of author intent.
