# Eight Sleep for Codex

Community adaptation of upstream 1.0.0 from `third_party/eight-sleep`. Original authors, assets and MIT license are retained.

Requires your Eight Sleep account and Pod. Confirm read_sleep/read_device access; write_device requires explicit agreement and a supported reconnect. Confirm every exact change on the user's own side; do not change a partner's side or offer diagnoses. Alarms/schedules depend on membership and the app timezone. Cover connectivity, online/away limits and queued offline alarm writes matter. Respect 60 calls/minute, 10 changes/5 minutes and 50 changes/day.

Register `<repository>/Codex` as the marketplace, then install `eight-sleep@cursor-plugins-codex`. [Upstream README](references/upstream-readme.md) is reference evidence; its client-specific setup instructions do not override the native qualifications above. Provider authentication and live tools are unverified.
